\begin{abstract}
We derive two local analytic results used in the archimedean part of
Levent Alp\"oge's proposed argument for the Riemann hypothesis and Dirichlet
GRH. The first gives real analyticity of smooth null solutions. The second
represents point evaluation by a compactly supported error kernel and an
integrable local fundamental kernel. Its support and quantifiers allow one
convolution kernel to reproduce every solution annihilated by a fixed
polynomial in the Laplacian.
\end{abstract}
