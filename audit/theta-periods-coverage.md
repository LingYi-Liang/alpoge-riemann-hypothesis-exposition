# Coverage of the condensed theta-period argument

The 12-page November 22 draft followed all nine sections of the 47-page exposition. It did not preserve every mathematical statement as written. The comparison below identifies corrections made in the 14-page revision and the places where a reader still needs the source. The central deduction in the revision uses the original arithmetic constructions. [Revised manuscript](../pdfs/theta-periods-abridged.pdf) · [Source exposition](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24413008)

## Corrections to the 12-page draft

| Location in the original | What changed and why |
|---|---|
| §3.4, p. 10 | For a coset with target mass $\lambda(c)$, its count must differ from $n_T\lambda(c)$ by at most $n_Tx^{-1+\epsilon}$. The old sentence compared the count directly to a proportion. For $o=2$ and $n_T=7$, exact target counts are 4 and 3. The revised text gives both the count hypothesis and the resulting Fourier inequality.
